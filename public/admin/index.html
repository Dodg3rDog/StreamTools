    Enter one phrase per line. Available placeholders: {issuer}, {target}, {channel}, {duration}, and {reason}.

    Duration: 30 seconds to 24 hours. Saved settings apply immediately to new confinements and persist across restarts. Existing confinements keep their scheduled release times. Built-in exemptions remain in effect.

    Channels and the confinement role still use server configuration.

    !eat accepts one or more mentioned users while everyone shares a voice channel. Add one duration such as 60s or 1m to override the default for everyone mentioned in that command. The same issuer can use !vomit or !release to release everyone they ate. Bots, administrators, the server owner, configured exempt roles, and unmanageable members are excluded.

    On release, Howler restores removed roles and returns members who remain in Tummy~Time to their original voice channel. Members who have already left receive a permanent-vore response. Failed role restorations retry every five minutes.
